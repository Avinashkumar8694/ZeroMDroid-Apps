# ZeroMDroid 1.3.2

Free with ads; a Pro licence removes the ads and the time limit. Files between your phone and your computer over Wi-Fi, USB or a QR code, shared whiteboards, camera to computer.

Pick the file for your system (the table in the [README](https://github.com/Avinashkumar8694/ZeroMDroid-Apps#download) says which), install it, and open ZeroMDroid on both devices. Every file's SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS`.

* macOS: `.dmg` (Apple silicon: `arm64`, Intel: `x64`)
* Windows: `Setup-…-x64.exe` for most PCs
* Linux: `.AppImage` (runs anywhere), `.deb`, `.tar.gz`
* Android: `.apk`
